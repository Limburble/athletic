# Athletic Sanctuary

register: product

## Product Purpose
A private, data-accumulating workout app that feels like a personal room, not a fitness tracker. It learns from history — streaks, session logs, check-ins — and generates intelligent workout suggestions. The emotional register is calm, private, editorial. The app should feel like something worth returning to.

## Users
Single user (Brayden). Power user who values craft. Expects the interface to be quiet and intentional — not gamified, not pushy. Mobile-first, 430px max-width.

## Brand Tone
Warm editorial minimalism. Think a well-worn journal or a Criterion film: deliberate, unhurried, precise. The copy is terse and italicized, not motivational. The design gives things room to breathe.

## Anti-references
- Fitness apps: bright primary colors, achievement badges, gamification, loud CTAs ("CRUSH IT")
- Generic dark-mode SaaS: blue-gray, Inter, dropdowns, modals
- Apple Health / Strava: data-dense, utilitarian, brand-neutral

## Design language keywords
Near-black parchment warmth · editorial serif (Newsreader) · film grain texture · breathing aura orbs · gold/sage/slate accents · breathing animations · lamplight glow · private sanctuary

## Strategic principles
1. The app should disappear into the experience — never fight the user
2. Every screen is a room, not a form
3. Data accumulates silently and is surfaced warmly, not as trophies
4. Quiet confidence over motivational noise
